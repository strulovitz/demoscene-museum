# Space Debris — music exhibit

**Music:** "Space Debris" by **Captain (Markus Kaarlonen)**
**Website:** https://markuskaarlonen.com/space-debris

All rights belong to Markus Kaarlonen. This famous Amiga module (ProTracker MOD,
4 channels) is included here as a tribute, with thanks to the composer for his
kind contribution to demoscene history. Please visit his page above and support
his work.

The music pillar in the museum Lobby plays this module: one channel per face.
The playback uses the museum's own reference MOD player (see
`bible/05c-mod-replayer.md`).
