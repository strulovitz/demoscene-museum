The Demoscene Museum

A walkable, three-dimensional museum of demoscene history — the computer-graphics and music scene of the Amiga, the PC and the demos of the 1990s — built entirely from scratch with a software raycaster, the way Wolfenstein 3D did it in 1992, with no engine and no libraries.

The works on the walls are not videos of old demos: they run live, right there on the wall, and every exhibit comes with its code, ported to Python, Java and JavaScript, and a lecture on how it works.

Music hangs in the museum too: Space Debris by Captain (Markus Kaarlonen) plays on a pillar in the Lobby, one channel of the song on each of its four faces, so you can walk around the music and watch it play.
